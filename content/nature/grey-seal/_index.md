Grey seals range widely across the northern Atlantic, from the United States to
Russia, and were estimated at about 632,000 in 2016.{{< cite "wiki" >}} They live on
rocks, islands and shoals near the shore and breed on beaches and remote
islands.{{< cite "wiki" >}} Record bulls reach about 3.3 m long.{{< cite "wiki" >}}
The species is listed as Least Concern.{{< cite "iucn" >}}

## In the Wadden Sea

Grey seals share the Wadden Sea with the smaller harbour seal. In 2025, 3,051 grey
seal pups and 12,064 moulting grey seals were counted there.{{< cite "whs" >}}

## What we do

Telling the two species apart, sexing them and judging their life stage from aerial
photos is expert, slow work. Our [Wadden Sea seal monitoring](/projects/wadden_sea_seal_monitoring/)
work does it automatically for every seal in a survey, and recognises individuals
across seasons, so researchers spend their time on the numbers rather than the
counting.
