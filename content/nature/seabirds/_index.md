Nearly 95% of seabirds breed in colonies,{{< cite "colony" >}} packing thousands of
nests onto a beach, a dune or an island. Along the Dutch coast, the Wadden Sea is a
stopover for 10 to 12 million migrating birds every year.{{< cite "whs" >}}

![A seabird colony seen from a drone: thousands of nesting birds between patches of dune vegetation](/images/projects/bird_flu_monitoring/cover.png)

## Under pressure

Colonies are also where disease spreads fastest. In the 2022 breeding season, highly
pathogenic avian influenza H5N1 swept through Sandwich tern colonies in the
Netherlands: 8,001 adults were found dead in the colonies, and another 1,600 outside
them, out of 18,151 breeding pairs.{{< cite "cdc" >}}

## What we do

Walking into a colony to count the dead disturbs nesting birds and spreads the risk.
With [AI-BIRD](/tools/ai-bird/), a drone flies over instead, and our models find
every bird, identify its species, sex and behaviour, and check whether it is alive,
turning a flight into a survey.{{< cite "aibird" >}} Read more about the
[bird flu monitoring project](/projects/bird_flu_monitoring/).
