Temperate forests grow between the tropics and the boreal north, at latitudes of 25
to 50 degrees in both hemispheres, and cover 25% of the world's forest
area.{{< cite "wiki" >}} Oak, beech, maple and birch share them with pine and
fir.{{< cite "wiki" >}}

## Why they matter

Much of Europe's temperate forest was cleared long ago for farms and
cities.{{< cite "wiki" >}} What remains wild is precious: Romania still holds 250,000
hectares of virgin forest, most of it in the Carpathians, and the Southern
Carpathians form Europe's largest continuous unfragmented forest.{{< cite "carp" >}}

## Under pressure

Logging, mining, hunting, pollution and habitat loss all weigh on these
forests,{{< cite "wiki" >}} and in dry summers, so does wildfire.

## What we do

With [Pyronear](/tools/pyronear/), cameras on hilltops watch the forest and flag
smoke early, so firefighters can reach a fire while it is still small. In the Carpathians, our [bear deterrence](/projects/carpathian-bear-deterrence/)
work keeps bears and farmers apart at the forest edge.
