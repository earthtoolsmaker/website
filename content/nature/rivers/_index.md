Freshwater ecosystems hold 41% of the world's known fish species.{{< cite "wiki" >}}
Rivers are also roads: salmon and trout hatch in their gravel, travel down them to
grow, and come back up them to spawn, and the nutrients they carry feed the forest
along the banks.{{< cite "salmon" >}}

## Under pressure

Freshwater vertebrate populations fell by 83% between 1970 and 2014, according to
the Living Planet Index.{{< cite "wiki" >}}
The causes are well known: overexploitation, pollution, dams and altered flows,
habitat destruction and invasive species.{{< cite "wiki" >}}

## What we do

You cannot manage a river without knowing what swims in it. [SalmonVision](/tools/salmonvision/)
counts and classifies migrating salmon from cameras, sonar and drones, our
[smolt sonar work](/projects/monitoring_smolt_salmon_migration_with_sonar/) follows
young salmon out to sea, and [trout identification](/projects/trout_identification/)
recognises individual westslope cutthroat trout in British Columbia's Elk River.
